What is the importance of ~37 GHz? What are its limits

Back: 
![[Pasted image 20250514145344.png|300]]
- 37 GHz, this channel correlates brightness temperature with the number of hydrometeors in the cloud (which correlates with precipitation from that cloud)
- Although bright over the SEA, over LAND it is hard to distinguish the brightness temp of precipitating clouds from the land itself (which is passively quite bright) limiting use case here

What is the importance of ~85 GHz? 

Back: 
![[Pasted image 20250514145808.png|500]]
- The ice in the cloud scatters this frequency, DECREASING brightness temperature and this property correlates with precipitation
- This can be seen over land unlike 37 Ghz

How can microwaves measure salinity, what's a major issue?

Back: 
- Emissivity of water varies with salt content -> brightness temp measures it
- Sea roughness (due to wind) can effect brightness, which needs to be accounted for
