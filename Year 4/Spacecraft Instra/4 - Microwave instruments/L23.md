How does SAR work?

Back: 
- By sending out many pulses, we detect their echoes a moment later. Each pulse's frequency is slightly altered due to Doppler shift, allowing us to distinguish between them. We can apply an FFT (Fast Fourier Transform) to separate these frequencies for detailed analysis.
- As the satellite moves, we continuously capture these echoes, allowing us to combine multiple measurements into a "synthetic aperture." This simulates a much larger antenna, greatly improving resolution while sacrificing some coverage area.
