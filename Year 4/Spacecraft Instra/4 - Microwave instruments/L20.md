What is an issue using IR to measure SST?

Back: 
- Sea surface temp can only be mesured to 0.1K (usually good enough) using IR
- IR is obstructed by clouds, hence we need to time average or filter out cloud blocked measurements (composite measurements can take months)
- Generally requires correction for the water present in the atmosphere
- Only measures the SURFACE
