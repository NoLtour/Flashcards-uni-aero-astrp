Define swath

Back: 
- The area imaged on the surface, is referred to as the swath.
- Imaging swaths for spaceborne sensors generally vary between tens and hundreds of kilometres wide.
- Depends on the across track scan angle of the sensor (for whiskbroom) or the width of the linear detector array (for a pushbroom sensor)

What is SLC? Why is it good/bad.

Back: 
- Scanning line correction
- By moving the scanner in a hourglass shape, we can compensate for the distortion that'd be seen from simple linear motion
- It adds mechanical complexity -> a possible point of failure

What is are disadvantages/advantages of a push broom scanner?

- dis - Calibration difficulty, each pixel needs to be calibrated else you'll see striping
- dis - Harder to expand swath width

How do we calculate dwell time on push-broom and whisk-broom (with a single celled scanner)?

Back: 
- For push broom: (dwell time) = (scan time per line)
- For whisk broom: (dwell time) = (scan time per line)/(cells per line)
