State some common features of spy satellites.

Back:

- Extreme flexibility in mirror angle - for taking images at unusual angles

START_CARD
Basic

State and briefly describe common cooling methods.

Back: 
- Passive - Radiators - panels radiating heat according to Stefan's Law 
- Passive - Stored cryogens - Dewars containing a cryogen such as liquid helium or solid neon may be used to achieve temperatures below those offered by radiators (heat is absorbed by either boiling or sublimation respectively)
- Active coolers - Stirling cycle - These coolers are based on causing a working gas to undergo a Stirling cycle, mechanical working of a gas (classical, what you find in a fridge)
- Active coolers - Pulse tube - Uses physical manipulation of gas, simular to stirling but with less moving parts
- Active coolers - Joule-Thompson -  These coolers work using the well known Joule-Thomson (Joule-Kelvin), effect. A gas is forced through a thermally isolated porous plug or throttle valve by a mechanical compressor unit leading to isenthalpic cooling. (irreversible process)
- Active coolers - Peltier effect coolers - These devices work on the same principle as the Seebeck effect, but in reverse: the creation of a temperature difference between two dissimilar metals by application of a current. Solid state, low efficiency, high min temp
