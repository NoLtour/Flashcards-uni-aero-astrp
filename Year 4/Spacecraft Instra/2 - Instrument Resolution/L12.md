Basic

What are desirable properties in a space mirror?

Back: 
- Low mass
- Easy to machine (we probably want to mill it to reduce weight)
- No thermal expansion
- How easy it is to polish
- Needs sufficient structural strength (especially for launch)
- Chemical inertness
- No degradation in the space environment
- We want its aperture to be large (Relie criterion) for better performance

END_CARD
