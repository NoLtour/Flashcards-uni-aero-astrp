What are the key considerations for signal strength with a whisk broom sensor?

Back: 
- Low dwell time because it's moving so fast
- Variable light-path, depending on altitude and FOV could have significant amounts of light scattering through atmosphere
- The passive source of illumination - time of day and frequency
- The altitude - linear square law
- The detectors bandwidth
- The IFOV per pixel (surface area emitting light per pixel)

What is TDI? Why do we use it?

Back: 
- Time delay integration
- As the image of the object moves past each line, each line captures a snapshot of the object.
- It effectively "increases dwell time" -> increases exposure -> improved __signal to noise ratio__ -> good for high res images
- Signal to noise ratio reduces with square root of TDI pixel rows
- It helps with calibration and can greatly reduce striping
- It does require very precise timing -> well suited for spacecraft
