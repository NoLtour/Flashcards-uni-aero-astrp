What does lidar measure?

Back: 
- Lidar uses lasers to measure distance, it also gets some information on reflectivity

What are the two types of lidar

Back: 
- Topographic lidar: typically uses a near-infrared laser to map the land
- Bathymetric lidar: uses water- penetrating green light to also measure seafloor and riverbed elevation

How do lidars process light?

Back: 
- They use lasers which emit light with very narrow bands
- To improve signal to noise they filter light that is outside this bands

START_CARD
Basic

State use cases of lidar.

Back: 
- Measuring the composition of the atmosphere (according to scattering)
- Measuring the thickness and height of clouds or specific aerosols
- Measuring the height of terrain
- Measuring the depth of water
- Measuring the height of underwater terrain
- Measuring the thickness of specific materials (some things other than water can be petrated)
- Note: thickness measurements generally require multiple frequencies of light
