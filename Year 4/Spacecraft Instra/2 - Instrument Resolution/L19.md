Basic

Compare infrared to microwave radiometry.

Back: 
- Infrared has much higher spatial resolution
- Infrared requires smaller instruments
- Infrared can be obscured by clouds but microwaves aren't (much)
- Infrared is more abundant, it can be analysed with narrower wavebands -> spectroscopy is possible
- Microwave sensing is less sensitive to time of day -> works well at night
- Microwaves can provide information about surface texture
- Microwaves can be penetrating which can provide information of subsurface features or surface under vegetation

What alternatives to satellite sea surface temp measurement exist, why might we want to use them for SST?

Back: 
- Boys and ships can be used to directly measure SST, these are point measurements (limited coverage) but highly accurate
- Satellites only measure the topmost layer of the seas temp (~0.1mm) which can be 1K different from the reading at 1m, boys can effectively measure at depth
- This difference can be significant for climatologists

How does SST vary with depth, at what depth does microwaves and IR measure? What depth are we most intrested in?

Back: 
- IR measures the "skin" temperature, which is microns deep
- Microwave measures the subskin temp which is 0.1mm deep
- We are most intrested in the 1m temperature
- Temperature generally drops with depth, but quickly reaches a steady state
