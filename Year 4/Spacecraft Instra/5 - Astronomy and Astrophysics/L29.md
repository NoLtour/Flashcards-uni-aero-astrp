What is the working principle of an optically pumped magnetometer?

Back: 
- These have a vapor chamber, in which a gas is bombarded with ionising radiation
- This causes the gas to become transparent to that radiation and can be detected
- By inducing a local AC at a specific frequency (determined by ambient field) the gas can become opaque again
- The specific AC needed can be used to get information about the ambient magnetic field
- Scalar measurement
