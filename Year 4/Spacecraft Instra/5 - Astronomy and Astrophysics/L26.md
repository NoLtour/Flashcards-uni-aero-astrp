What are the differences between Refractor and Reflector Telescopes?

Back: 

Refractor Telescopes:
- Use lenses to gather and focus light.
- First type of telescope, designed by the Dutch and used by Galileo.
- Heavy lenses that are difficult to manufacture.
- Prone to chromatic aberration (color distortion caused by light wavelengths bending differently).

Reflector Telescopes:
- Use mirrors instead of lenses to focus light.
- Solves chromatic aberration by reflecting all wavelengths to a common focus.
- Spherical mirrors can cause spherical aberration (light rays do not converge properly).
- Overcome by using parabolic or hyperbolic mirrors to focus all light accurately.
