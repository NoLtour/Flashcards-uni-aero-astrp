How can we detect gamma rays with a telescope?

Back: 
- Directly using a solid stat detector, where impact causes an electric current
- Indirectly by using scintillators where a lower energy light is emitted by some material in response to gamma rays, which is then pushed through a photo-multiplying tube and to a detector
