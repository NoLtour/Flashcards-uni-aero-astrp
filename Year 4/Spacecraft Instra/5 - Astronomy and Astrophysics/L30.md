How does a RPA work?

Back: 
- Retarding potential analyser, uses a mesh to repel incident particles away from some path
- If it has enough energy it makes it past this path and hits the detector
- Is known as a high pass filter since you need above X power to make it to the end

How does a mirror analyser work?

Back: 
- Opposite of RPA, detectors are placed to the side, if the particles have LOW enough energy to be deflected into the detector they are detected
- Low pass filter
