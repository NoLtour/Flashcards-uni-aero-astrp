- this is very hard to do, so focal lengths are large
- hence the instrument ends up being quite long

How can we measure gamma rays

Back: 
- coded aperture mask can help measure X rays and gamma rays (mostly gamma)
- due to their high energy we can't use traditional focusing methods like lenses, instead we use coded apertures
- the mask casts a shadow pattern from any beam sources, this is effectively a convolution of incoming point sources and the mask
- Though maths and using knowledge of the masks shape we can deconvolve the pattern and get an image (the mask is made of lead)
